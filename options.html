JournalLens — Settings
No third-party databases: Scopus, Clarivate and Crossref only

My name as it appears on papers

One variant per line (given name then family name), e.g. Ahmad Al-Saleh or A. Al-Saleh. Matching uses the family name plus the first initial.

Google Scholar

JournalLens runs by itself on Google Scholar: profile pages get a statistics panel and a badge row under every paper; search and "Cited by" pages get a badge row under every result.

Official Impact Factor file (JCR)

The Journal Impact Factor belongs to Clarivate and is not published for free; extensions that "show" it without a subscription copy unofficial lists, which is why they are wrong. JournalLens shows it only from the official JCR export:

  1. Open jcr.clarivate.com → Journals through your institution.
  2. Choose the JCR year and editions (SCIE, SSCI, AHCI, ESCI), then Export → CSV (if you get Excel, save it as CSV UTF-8).
  3. Import it here. The file is read locally and sent nowhere.

Cache

Scopus results are kept for 7 days, Master Journal List results for 30 days and paper matches for 30 days. "Refresh" bypasses the cache.
